## Overall Impression

Disciplined, unusually accessible MVP scaffold with a real component system that is also a generic template with Portuguese labels, shipping with one role area crashing. Biggest opportunity: make the three areas structurally different and add domain-shaped components.

## What's Working
1. Deep, consistent component system (ui.jsx primitives used uniformly).
2. Above-average accessibility foundation (skip link, focus management, aria state, reduced-motion, auto-scrolling nav).
3. Empty states that explain why and offer a next step.
